## Item 1. Business Overview
Lumen Media Group (NASDAQ/NYSE: LMDG) operates in the Media & Entertainment sector. Lumen Media Group produces and distributes filmed entertainment and operates a direct-to-consumer streaming service, Lumen+, with 58 million global subscribers at fiscal year end. For fiscal year 2025, the Company reported total revenue of $9.3 billion and net income of $212 million (-8% year over year revenue change). The Company employed approximately 16,900 people as of fiscal year end and invested $340 million in research and development during the year.

## Item 1A. Risk Factors
Operational and Regulatory Risk. Linear television advertising revenue declined 21% in FY2025 as advertisers continued shifting spend to streaming and social platforms, and the Company recorded a $410 million impairment charge against the carrying value of two regional cable news networks. The Company continues to monitor this matter and has implemented corrective actions as described in Item 7. Investors should consider this risk factor alongside general risks common to the Media & Entertainment industry, including competitive pressure, input cost inflation, regulatory change, and macroeconomic cyclicality.
