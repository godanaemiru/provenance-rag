## Item 1. Business Overview
Cornerstone Realty Trust (NASDAQ/NYSE: CRLT) operates in the Commercial Real Estate (REIT) sector. Cornerstone Realty Trust is a REIT owning 214 office and mixed-use properties concentrated in gateway US cities, with a portfolio focus on Class A office space. For fiscal year 2025, the Company reported total revenue of $3.4 billion and net income of $590 million (+2% year over year revenue change). ## Item 1A. Risk Factors
Operational and Regulatory Risk. Portfolio-wide office occupancy fell to 79.4% at fiscal year end from 85.1% a year earlier, and the Company recorded a $310 million impairment on three San Francisco office properties whose appraised values declined amid persistent remote-work trends. The Company continues to monitor this matter and has implemented corrective actions as described in Item 7. Investors should consider this risk factor alongside general risks common to the Commercial Real Estate (REIT) industry, including competitive pressure, input cost inflation, regulatory change, and macroeconomic cyclicality.
