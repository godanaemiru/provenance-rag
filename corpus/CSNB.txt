## Item 1A. Risk Factors
Operational and Regulatory Risk. Commercial real estate loans secured by office properties represented 9.8% of Cascade's loan portfolio at fiscal year end; the Bank increased its allowance for credit losses on this segment by $84 million during FY2025 due to rising office vacancy rates in Portland and Seattle. The Company continues to monitor this matter and has implemented corrective actions as described in Item 7.
