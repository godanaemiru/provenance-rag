## Item 1. Business Overview
Meridian Biopharma, Inc. (NASDAQ/NYSE: MRBP) operates in the Pharmaceuticals sector. Meridian Biopharma researches and commercializes therapies for autoimmune and inflammatory diseases, with its flagship product Cerivant (brizukimab) approved in the US, EU, and Japan for moderate-to-severe plaque psoriasis. For fiscal year 2025, the Company reported total revenue of $6.35 billion and net income of $-210 million (+3% year over year revenue change). The Company employed approximately 9,700 people as of fiscal year end and invested $2.41 billion in research and development during the year.

## Item 1A. Risk Factors
Operational and Regulatory Risk. In August 2025 the FDA issued a Complete Response Letter for Meridian's supplemental application seeking to expand Cerivant's label to pediatric patients aged 6-11, citing insufficient long-term safety data from the PEDIGREE-2 trial; the Company does not expect a refiled submission before mid-2026. The Company continues to monitor this matter and has implemented corrective actions as described in Item 7. Investors should consider this risk factor alongside general risks common to the Pharmaceuticals industry, including competitive pressure, input cost inflation, regulatory change, and macroeconomic cyclicality.

Fiscal 2025 revenue of $6.35 billion compared to the prior fiscal year reflected a change of +3%.
