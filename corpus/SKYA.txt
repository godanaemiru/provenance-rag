## Item 1A. Risk Factors
Operational and Regulatory Risk. A software defect in Skyline's crew-scheduling system in December 2025 caused the cancellation of approximately 2,800 flights over five days during the holiday travel period, resulting in an estimated $410 million impact to revenue and customer-compensation costs. The Company continues to monitor this matter and has implemented corrective actions as described in Item 7. Investors should consider this risk factor alongside general risks common to the Passenger Airlines industry, including competitive pressure, input cost inflation, regulatory change, and macroeconomic cyclicality.
