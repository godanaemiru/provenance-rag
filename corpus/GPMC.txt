## Item 1. Business Overview
Granite Peak Mining Co. (NASDAQ/NYSE: GPMC) operates in the Metals & Mining sector. Granite Peak Mining extracts and processes copper, lithium, and molybdenum from four open-pit mines in Nevada, Arizona, and Chile. For fiscal year 2025, the Company reported total revenue of $10.6 billion and net income of $1.84 billion (+15% year over year revenue change). The Company employed approximately 11,200 people as of fiscal year end and invested $88 million in research and development during the year.

## Item 1A. Risk Factors
Operational and Regulatory Risk. A tailings-dam stability review at the Company's Cerro Alto facility in Chile, prompted by a Chilean regulatory audit in FY2025, required $46 million of remediation work and a temporary 25% reduction in processing throughput for 10 weeks while reinforcement work was completed. The Company continues to monitor this matter and has implemented corrective actions as described in Item 7. Investors should consider this risk factor alongside general risks common to the Metals & Mining industry, including competitive pressure, input cost inflation, regulatory change, and macroeconomic cyclicality.

## Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis (MD&A)
Fiscal 2025 revenue of $10.6 billion compared to the prior fiscal year reflected a change of +15%. Copper prices averaged 22% higher year over year amid tight global supply, driving the majority of revenue and net income growth despite flat production volumes. Management believes the Company's underlying operating trends remain consistent with its multi-year strategic plan, and it continues to monitor the risk factor described in Item 1A for further financial statement impact in future periods.
