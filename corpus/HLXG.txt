## Item 1. Business Overview
Helix Genomics, Inc. (NASDAQ/NYSE: HLXG) operates in the Biotechnology sector. Helix Genomics develops gene-editing therapies for inherited blood disorders, with its lead product Zenovex (an ex vivo CRISPR-based therapy for sickle cell disease) approved in the United States in FY2024. For fiscal year 2025, the Company reported total revenue of $1.42 billion and net income of $-380 million (+46% year over year revenue change). The Company employed approximately 3,900 people as of fiscal year end and invested $740 million in research and development during the year.

## Item 1A. Risk Factors
Operational and Regulatory Risk. One of 340 patients treated commercially with Zenovex since launch experienced a delayed-onset secondary blood cancer in FY2025; the FDA has requested an updated long-term safety monitoring plan, and the Company voluntarily paused new patient enrollment for 45 days while the case was investigated, concluding it was unrelated to the therapy's editing mechanism. The Company continues to monitor this matter and has implemented corrective actions as described in Item 7. Investors should consider this risk factor alongside general risks common to the Biotechnology industry, including competitive pressure, input cost inflation, regulatory change, and macroeconomic cyclicality.
