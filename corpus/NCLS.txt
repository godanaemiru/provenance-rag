## Item 1. Business Overview
Nimbus Cloud Systems, Inc. (NASDAQ/NYSE: NCLS) operates in the Enterprise Cloud Software sector. Nimbus Cloud Systems develops multi-tenant cloud infrastructure and data orchestration software for mid-market enterprises, serving customers across financial services, retail, and healthcare through its Nimbus Fabric platform. For fiscal year 2025, the Company reported total revenue of $4.82 billion and net income of $612 million (+18% year over year revenue change). The Company employed approximately 14,300 people as of fiscal year end and invested $891 million in research and development during the year.

## Item 1A. Risk Factors
Operational and Regulatory Risk. A March 2025 configuration error in the Nimbus Fabric access-control layer exposed metadata (not customer content) for approximately 40,000 tenant accounts for 6 hours before containment; the Company incurred $14 million in remediation and customer credits and now requires dual-approval for all production access-control changes. The Company continues to monitor this matter and has implemented corrective actions as described in Item 7. Investors should consider this risk factor alongside general risks common to the Enterprise Cloud Software industry, including competitive pressure, input cost inflation, regulatory change, and macroeconomic cyclicality.

## Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis (MD&A)
Fiscal 2025 revenue of $4.82 billion compared to the prior fiscal year reflected a change of +18%. Revenue growth was driven by a 24% increase in Nimbus Fabric seat licenses and the acquisition of Ledgerline Analytics in Q2 FY2025, which added $310 million in annualized recurring revenue. Management believes the Company's underlying operating trends remain consistent with its multi-year strategic plan, and it continues to monitor the risk factor described in Item 1A for further financial statement impact in future periods.
