## Item 1. Business Overview
Bluepeak Insurance Holdings (NASDAQ/NYSE: BPIH) operates in the Property & Casualty Insurance sector. Bluepeak Insurance Holdings underwrites homeowners, auto, and commercial property insurance in 34 US states, with concentrated exposure in Gulf Coast and California wildfire-prone regions. For fiscal year 2025, the Company reported total revenue of $7.6 billion and net income of $-95 million (+4% year over year revenue change). ## Item 1A. Risk Factors
Operational and Regulatory Risk. Hurricane Elva made landfall near Mobile, Alabama in September 2025, generating estimated gross claims of $1.1 billion for the Company, of which $740 million was ceded to reinsurance partners, resulting in a net underwriting loss for the property segment. The Company continues to monitor this matter and has implemented corrective actions as described in Item 7. Fiscal 2025 revenue of $7.6 billion compared to the prior fiscal year reflected a change of +4%.
