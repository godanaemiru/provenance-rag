## Item 1. Business Overview
Sentinel Cyber Defense Corp. (NASDAQ/NYSE: SNTC) operates in the Cybersecurity Software sector. Sentinel Cyber Defense provides endpoint detection, identity threat protection, and managed security monitoring to enterprise and government customers. For fiscal year 2025, the Company reported total revenue of $3.28 billion and net income of $340 million (+21% year over year revenue change). The Company employed approximately 7,800 people as of fiscal year end and invested $620 million in research and development during the year.

## Item 1A. Risk Factors
Operational and Regulatory Risk. A supply-chain compromise of a widely used open-source logging library, disclosed industry-wide in FY2025, required Sentinel to patch and re-certify 96% of its customer-deployed sensor agents within a 30-day emergency window; the Company identified no evidence that its own systems or customer data were compromised. The Company continues to monitor this matter and has implemented corrective actions as described in Item 7. Investors should consider this risk factor alongside general risks common to the Cybersecurity Software industry, including competitive pressure, input cost inflation, regulatory change, and macroeconomic cyclicality.
