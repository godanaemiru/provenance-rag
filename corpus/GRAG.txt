## Item 1. Business Overview
Greenfield Agritech Corporation (NASDAQ/NYSE: GRAG) operates in the Agriculture & Crop Science sector. Greenfield Agritech develops seed genetics, crop protection chemicals, and precision-agriculture software for row-crop farmers across North and South America. For fiscal year 2025, the Company reported total revenue of $13.8 billion and net income of $1.11 billion (+1% year over year revenue change). The Company employed approximately 24,300 people as of fiscal year end and invested $1.05 billion in research and development during the year.

## Item 1A. Risk Factors
Operational and Regulatory Risk. A widespread drought across Argentina and southern Brazil reduced FY2025 soybean seed volumes by 9% in the Company's South American segment, and the Company extended $210 million of additional grower credit to affected customers to support next-season purchases. The Company continues to monitor this matter and has implemented corrective actions as described in Item 7. Investors should consider this risk factor alongside general risks common to the Agriculture & Crop Science industry, including competitive pressure, input cost inflation, regulatory change, and macroeconomic cyclicality.

## Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis (MD&A)
