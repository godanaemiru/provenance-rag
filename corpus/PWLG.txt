## Item 1. Business Overview
Pathway Logistics Corporation (NASDAQ/NYSE: PWLG) operates in the Freight & Logistics sector. Pathway Logistics operates a nationwide network of truckload, less-than-truckload, and warehousing services, along with a growing parcel-delivery division serving e-commerce retailers. For fiscal year 2025, the Company reported total revenue of $19.7 billion and net income of $880 million (+5% year over year revenue change). The Company employed approximately 58,000 people as of fiscal year end and invested $410 million in research and development during the year.

## Item 1A. Risk Factors
Operational and Regulatory Risk. A ransomware intrusion detected in the Company's warehouse-management systems in October 2025 disrupted order fulfillment at 19 distribution centers for approximately 60 hours; Pathway did not pay the ransom and restored operations from backups, incurring $37 million in incident-response and lost-throughput costs. The Company continues to monitor this matter and has implemented corrective actions as described in Item 7. Investors should consider this risk factor alongside general risks common to the Freight & Logistics industry, including competitive pressure, input cost inflation, regulatory change, and macroeconomic cyclicality.

## Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis (MD&A)
Fiscal 2025 revenue of $19.7 billion compared to the prior fiscal year reflected a change of +5%.
