## Item 1. Business Overview
Everwell Consumer Products (NASDAQ/NYSE: EVWL) operates in the Consumer Packaged Goods sector. Everwell Consumer Products manufactures household cleaning, personal care, and over-the-counter health products sold under 22 brands in more than 90 countries. For fiscal year 2025, the Company reported total revenue of $21.5 billion and net income of $2.63 billion (+3% year over year revenue change). The Company employed approximately 47,000 people as of fiscal year end and invested $580 million in research and development during the year.

## Item 1A. Risk Factors
Operational and Regulatory Risk. A voluntary recall of approximately 3.2 million units of the Company's Everwell Kids liquid multivitamin was issued in FY2025 after routine testing detected elevated vitamin A levels in a subset of production lots from the Company's Greenville, South Carolina facility. The Company continues to monitor this matter and has implemented corrective actions as described in Item 7. Investors should consider this risk factor alongside general risks common to the Consumer Packaged Goods industry, including competitive pressure, input cost inflation, regulatory change, and macroeconomic cyclicality.

## Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis (MD&A)
Fiscal 2025 revenue of $21.5 billion compared to the prior fiscal year reflected a change of +3%. Volume growth in emerging markets, particularly India and Indonesia, offset continued private-label competitive pressure in North American home care categories. Management believes the Company's underlying operating trends remain consistent with its multi-year strategic plan, and it continues to monitor the risk factor described in Item 1A for further financial statement impact in future periods.
