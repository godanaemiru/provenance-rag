## Item 1. Business Overview
Titan Industrial Works (NASDAQ/NYSE: TIWK) operates in the Heavy Industrial Manufacturing sector. Titan Industrial Works manufactures heavy construction equipment, mining machinery, and industrial engines, distributing through an independent dealer network across 140 countries. For fiscal year 2025, the Company reported total revenue of $16.4 billion and net income of $1.55 billion (+6% year over year revenue change). The Company employed approximately 38,200 people as of fiscal year end and invested $712 million in research and development during the year.

## Item 1A. Risk Factors
Operational and Regulatory Risk. A labor strike at the Company's Peoria, Illinois engine-manufacturing complex lasted 37 days in FY2025, reducing engine shipment volumes by an estimated 12% for the fiscal third quarter and resulting in $58 million of incremental labor and overtime costs to recover backlog. The Company continues to monitor this matter and has implemented corrective actions as described in Item 7. Investors should consider this risk factor alongside general risks common to the Heavy Industrial Manufacturing industry, including competitive pressure, input cost inflation, regulatory change, and macroeconomic cyclicality.

## Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis (MD&A)
Fiscal 2025 revenue of $16.4 billion compared to the prior fiscal year reflected a change of +6%. Strong demand for mining equipment, particularly in copper and lithium extraction markets, drove order backlog to a record $24.6 billion at fiscal year end. Management believes the Company's underlying operating trends remain consistent with its multi-year strategic plan, and it continues to monitor the risk factor described in Item 1A for further financial statement impact in future periods.
